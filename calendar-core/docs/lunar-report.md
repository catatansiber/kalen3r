## Chosen rule

`tithiOn(date) = tithiOf(floor(D / 12 deg) + 1)`. D is the **mean** elongation at
`TITHI_REFERENCE = 12:54 WITA`, the centre of the zero-mismatch window.

Result: **0 tithi mismatches and 0 Purnama/Tilem mismatches on all 212 golden days** (2024 H1: 0; 2026-11: 0).

Justification:
- **Why mean elongation.** A rule on the true elongation cannot reproduce the golden data at any hour.
  The true tithi varies in length (about 20-27 h), so a once-a-day sample gives irregular skips *and*
  repeats. The golden has no repeats, and its skips are evenly spaced (63 days). That is the behaviour
  of a *mean* (arithmetic) lunar calendar: the Balinese pengalantaka counts ngunalatri every 63 days.
  The mean elongation advances 12.19 deg a day, so it never repeats and skips every 62.9 days.
  This was a change of algorithm, not a patch: the true-elongation rules left at least 55 mismatches.
- **Why 12:54.** The hour only sets the phase of the arithmetic cycle, which is in effect an epoch
  constant, and it is fitted. It is not physically motivated, and 06:00 (sunrise) gives 54 mismatches
  under the mean model. 12:54 is the centre of the 19-minute window that fits; either window edge is
  only 9 minutes away.
- **Overfitting caveat.** Two choices were fitted to 212 days: the model family (true or mean) and one
  time constant. The fit is pinned mainly by the 3 skip dates and 14 Purnama/Tilem transitions.
  A strict 63-day arithmetic rule also fits all 212 days with 0 mismatches (best fit: phase 54, n0 19).
  **The golden data cannot tell the mean-elongation rule from a strict 63-day rule.**

## How skipped and repeated tithis are represented

- `tithiOn(date)` returns exactly one tithi: the one current at the reference time.
- A skipped tithi shows only as a step of 2 between consecutive days. `Lunar.skippedTithiOn(date)` returns
  the tithi skipped on entering `date`, or null. Example: 2024-01-10 returns PANGELONG 14.
- Under the chosen rule, repeated tithis (a step of 0) never occur. `LunarTest` asserts this for 2024-2035.
- A two-number golden cell such as "2/3" (none so far) counts as a match if `tithiOn` equals either number.
  Corrections are Task 7's job.
